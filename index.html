M-Park - Smart Parking System

Park smart. Contact the owner in seconds.

One QR sticker on the car. Anyone who needs to move the car can scan it, verify with OTP, and reach the owner by call or text. No phone number is shown openly.

How M-Park works

Step 1

Order a sticker

A car owner orders an M-Park QR sticker from the landing page and registers the vehicle.

Step 2

Verify with OTP

Mobile number and QR code are verified with OTP, then the sticker is activated.

Step 3

Scan the sticker

A visitor scans the sticker on the car and enters name and number with OTP verification.

Step 4

Contact the owner

The visitor can call or text the owner, or use the emergency contact if needed.

Demo data notice

All names, phone numbers, plates, QR codes, plans, and prices in these dashboards are demo data saved in your browser (localStorage). There is no backend, no real payment, and no real SMS or OTP service in this build. Demo prices are in PKR and are placeholders until the client confirms real plan prices.
